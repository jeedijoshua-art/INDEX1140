Recipe 1: Chicken Fry

Ingredients:

  • 500g chicken
  • 2 tbsp chili powder
  • 1 tbsp turmeric
  • 1 tbsp salt
  • Oil for frying

Steps:

  1. Marinate chicken with spices.
  2. Heat oil in pan.
  3. Deep fry until golden brown.
  4. Serve hot.

Recipe 2: Gulab Jamun

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup milk powder
  • 2 tbsp flour
  • 1 tbsp ghee
  • Sugar syrup
  • Oil for frying

Steps:

  1. Mix ingredients into dough.
  2. Shape into small balls.
  3. Fry until golden brown.
  4. Soak in sugar syrup.

Recipe 3: Mango Lassi

Ingredients:

  • 2 ripe mangoes
  • 1 cup yogurt
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • Ice cubes

Steps:

  1. Peel and chop mangoes.
  2. Blend mangoes with yogurt, sugar, and ice.
  3. Serve chilled in tall glasses.

Recipe 4: Chicken Cutlet

Ingredients:

  • 500g boneless chicken
  • 1 cup yogurt
  • Indian masalas
  • Maida flour
  • Oil
  • Eggs
  • Bread crumbs
  • Parmesan cheese
  • Italian herbs

Steps:

  1. Dredge chicken in flour, then egg, then breadcrumbs.
  2. Rest for 10 min before frying.
  3. Fry or air fry until golden brown.
