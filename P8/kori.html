Karunya University Buildings Overview

Karunya University Main Campus View

Central Building

This is the central building where research and other activities take place.

This is the most attractive building on campus and brings a feeling of entering a new environment.

CTC: Computer Technology Center

This is the place where all the connections of Karunya University converge. All the connections across campus run through these 3 buildings:

  • CTC-1: At the top of Central Library (3rd floor)
  • CTC-2: After the Central Library, another building
  • CTC-3: Near the Main Building

Computer Science Engineering Block

This is where all CSE students study. This block has 3 floors and is very close to the hills of Sirivani.

Location: This block is situated at the south corner of Karunya University. It is somewhat far away but easily accessible.

Electronics and Communication Engineering Block

This is a very large building and houses CTC-3.

Location: Situated to the right side of the Main Building.

Mechanical Engineering Department

The Mechanical Engineering department focuses on the design, development, and maintenance of machines and mechanical systems. It covers thermodynamics, manufacturing, fluid mechanics, materials science, and robotics.

It prepares students for careers in industries such as automotive, aerospace, energy, and manufacturing.

Location: Located at the East Side of Karunya University, near Emmanuel Auditorium.

Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning Department

This department specializes in developing intelligent systems capable of learning, adaptation, and decision making. Focus areas include data science, neural networks, natural language processing, and computer vision to solve real-world problems.

Location: Located after the ECE Block.

Aerospace Engineering Department

This department focuses on the design, development, and maintenance of aircraft and spacecraft, including aerodynamics, propulsion, materials science, and flight mechanics.

Location: In front of the Mechanical Engineering Department Block. The Aerospace and Mechanical Departments are opposite to each other.

Agricultural Engineering Department

This department applies engineering principles to improve farming efficiency, including irrigation, farm machinery, soil management, and post-harvest technology.

Location: On the way to the CSE block. When walking to the CSE block, it is on your left.

Food Processing Department

This department deals with techniques for transforming raw ingredients into safe, high-quality food products, focusing on preservation, packaging, and quality assurance.

Location: Located between the AI&ML block and ECE block.
